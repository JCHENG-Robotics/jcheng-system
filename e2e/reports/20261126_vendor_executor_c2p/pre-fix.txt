Pre-fix observation (2026-11-26, before R8)
============================================

The test `test_stop_before_send_does_not_publish` failed with:

    AssertionError: ['snapshot', 'hold', 'send_step', 'hold']
    assert 'send_step' not in ['snapshot', 'hold', 'send_step', 'hold']

Interpretation: the execute thread was paused inside
`HardwareExecutor.execute` after `_active` was set and before `send_step`.
`stop()` set the stop event, latched safety and published the hold. The
execute thread then continued and called `send_step` **after** the hold, and
the loop published a second hold before returning `ABORTED`.

After R8 the same interleaving records `['snapshot', 'hold', 'hold']` and no
`send_step`; the command is not published after the accepted stop.
